=============================================
  TEST SCENARIOS
=============================================

🟢 NORMAL (0-699):
   - Potentiometer: center (~512)
   - Green LED: ON
   - Serial: "Alert Level: NORMAL"

🟡 WARNING (700-899):
   - Potentiometer: 75% (~770)
   - Green LED: BLINKING
   - Serial: "Alert Level: WARNING [CHANGED!]"

🔴 CRITICAL (900-1023):
   - Potentiometer: maximum (~1000)
   - Red LED: BLINKING
   - Serial: "*** ALERT: CRITICAL! ***"
